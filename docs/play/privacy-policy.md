# Zham privacy policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-15_

Zham is a day planner and time tracker for Android. This policy explains what happens to your
information when you use it.

## The short version

Zham keeps everything on your phone. It has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no
advertising and no way to send data anywhere: the app does not ask for internet access.

## What the app stores

The things you enter are saved in a database and a settings file inside the app's private storage
on your phone:

- categories, projects and goals you create;
- time records (what you did, when it started and ended, notes and descriptions);
- planned blocks, their repeat rules, reminders and which ones you ticked off;
- your settings (theme, first day of the week, 12 or 24 hour clock).

Nobody but you, and apps that you give access to your phone's files, can read this. The developer
cannot see it.

## Notifications and alarms

If you turn on a reminder for a planned block, Zham asks for permission to show notifications and
sets an alarm on your phone for the reminder time. On newer Android versions it can also ask for
the "alarms and reminders" permission so reminders arrive on the minute. Both are used only for
your reminders. You can refuse either; the app then works without them.

## Files you export or import

"Back up everything" and "Export records to CSV" write a file to a place that you choose. "Restore
or import" reads a file that you choose. Zham does not send these files anywhere. What you do with
an exported file afterwards is up to you.

## Backups made by Android

Android can include an app's data in the automatic backup of your phone to your own Google
account, if you have backup turned on in your phone's settings. That backup is made and handled by
Android and Google under your account, not by Zham, and you can turn it off in your phone's
settings.

## Children

Zham is a general productivity tool and is not directed at children. It collects no personal
information from anyone.

## Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android's app settings, and everything it stored on
your phone is deleted. Files you exported yourself stay where you put them until you delete them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date.
