## English (default)

**App name** (30 characters at most)

    Zham: Day Planner & Time Log

**Short description** (80 characters at most)

    Plan your day in blocks, track what you really did, and compare the two.

**Full description** (4000 characters at most)

    Zham shows one thing clearly: the day you planned next to the day you lived.

    The day is a single hour ruler with two lanes. On the left, outlined, is what you planned. On
    the right, filled in, is what you actually did. The gap between the lanes is the point.

    PLAN
    • Drop a block on any hour: title, description, category, project, start, end.
    • Repeat it once, every day, on chosen weekdays, or every month.
    • Set an optional reminder - at the start, or from 5 minutes to a day before.
    • Tick a block off and Zham records exactly the time it reserved.

    TRACK
    • Start a timer in one tap; the ring and the button take the colour of your category.
    • Or log time by hand with a number pad: type 1, 3, 2, 0 and it reads 13:20.
    • Work spanning midnight is handled; overlapping records are counted once.

    SEE
    • A week view in the same two lanes.
    • Reports for a day, week, month, year or any range, with a donut that starts fully grey and
      fills with colour as you track. The grey is time nobody tracked.
    • Filter by category; open any goal to see every record it is made of.

    ORGANISE
    • Categories hold projects. Goals can follow a whole category, one project, or one title.
    • 46 icons and 24 colours.

    PRIVATE BY DESIGN
    • No account, no ads, no analytics. Zham does not even ask for internet access.
    • Everything stays on your phone. Back up to a file you control and restore it any time.

    Light, dark and AMOLED themes. 12 or 24 hour clock. You choose the first day of the week.
